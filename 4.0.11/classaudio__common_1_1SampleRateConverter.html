Convert interleaved audio samples to a different sample rate.

The converter keeps one frame of look-ahead between calls. Call flush() after the final input block to retrieve the retained final frame.

Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentif a rate or channel count is not positive, or if the input does not contain complete frames.
